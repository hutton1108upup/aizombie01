---
title: Cookie Policy
description: Cookies used by the pre-launch preview
date: 2026-10-10
---

The preview may store theme and language preferences in browser storage. Account services use authentication cookies when enabled. Analytics and chat are not loaded unless their project identifiers are configured. There is no cookie consent or rejection control in this preview; browser settings can clear stored cookies and preferences. Actual third-party providers and purposes must be disclosed before their integrations are enabled.
