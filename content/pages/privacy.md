## Photo previews
The generator decodes, checks and resizes photos locally in your browser. It does not upload them, create a video, or store them on our server. Preview images live only in this tab and are cleared on reload or close.

## Accounts and operational data
When account services are enabled, authentication processes your name, email, login and session information. Hosting may process request metadata such as IP addresses and error logs to operate the service. Never send passwords, payment details or original photo bytes in support requests.

## Analytics
The production website uses Google Analytics 4 to measure public-page views and traffic sources, and Microsoft Clarity for clicks, scrolling, heatmaps and session recordings. Providers may process device/browser information, approximate location, identifiers and cookies to help us improve the preview. Our GA4 page-view events omit URL query strings and fragments and reduce referrer URLs to their origin. Google signals and advertising personalization are disabled in the tag configuration. Local and Workers preview hosts do not load these integrations.

The photo-preview form and its child content and images are explicitly masked for Clarity. We do not send photo bytes, filenames, email addresses or account identifiers as custom analytics events. Google Search Console provides aggregated search and indexing reports; it does not add a visitor tracking script. There is no site-provided consent or rejection control, and we do not claim that users have opted in. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Microsoft's privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

## Future generation
Before uploads or paid generation open, this policy will identify the video service provider, the data sent to it, retention and deletion rules, and a verified support contact. There is currently no promise of a 30-day deletion period or a provider non-training policy.
