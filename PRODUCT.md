# AI Zombie Video

## Current product
A pre-launch AI zombie video concept built on TanStack Start and Cloudflare Workers. The current generator validates and resizes photos locally; it does not upload, generate, charge or claim playable sample results.

## Available foundation
Authenticated, owner-scoped D1 video history; private R2 playback/download routes; soft deletion preserving accounting records; credit grant/debit/refund constraints; proposed shared credit-pack pricing; project legal pages and SEO routes.

## Release boundary
The video provider, private upload pipeline, queue, atomic reservation, signed credit-pack checkout, retention cleanup and real E2E validation are pending. Template AI and subscription server functions are closed through prelaunch middleware. Paid deployment is blocked by launch:check. A separately checked public preview closes all stateful endpoints before any database or provider access.

Do not claim pet fine-tuning, a finished video, an automatic refund, a fixed rendering time, or a provider retention/training policy until verified. Use only authorized inputs and validated real samples.
